Street football · 5-a-side · any phone

How to play

Phone: turn sideways. Left thumb moves. SHOOT (hold for power), PASS, TACKLE (tap = slide, hold = sprint), MARK (hold to shadow your man), CALL (ask for the ball).

Laptop: WASD or arrows move · J/Space pass · K shoot (hold) · L tackle · Shift sprint · X mark (hold) · C call for ball.

The ball bounces off the walls, no throw-ins. Bots fill any empty spot.
